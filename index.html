Current Research

Cross-Embodiment Adaptation of VLA Policies: Reproducing DART on Franka Panda → xArm7Ongoing
Fall 2026 – Present

I am reproducing DART (ECCV 2026), which adapts a Vision-Language-Action policy to a new robot from a single demonstration using weight arithmetic between source- and target-finetuned models. Starting from a π0.5 policy trained on the Franka Panda (LIBERO-Spatial), I generated a one-shot xArm7 demonstration with MimicGen-style trajectory retargeting, fine-tuned source and target models, and evaluated the composed policy on the xArm7 across held-out tasks.
